Speakers

Keynote Speakers

SP

Samuel O. Poore, MD, PhD

Opening Keynote · Chair, Division of Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery, University of Wisconsin–Madison

Co-Director of the UW Microsurgery and Regenerative Medicine Laboratory. Author of more than 150 peer-reviewed publications; led microsurgical training programs across Vietnam, Egypt, Rwanda, South Africa, Nicaragua, the Dominican Republic, and Panama.

PN

Peter Nthumba, MD, MSc

Keynote 2 · Head, Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery, AIC Kijabe Hospital, Kenya

Vice-Chair of Global Health, Vanderbilt University Medical Center. Founder of EACH Research and leader of the CIEPS Virtual Global Surgery Research Lab; recipient of the Noordhoff Humanitarian Award from ASPS.

FN

Faustin Ntirenganya, MD

Closing Keynote — Lessons from Global Plastic Surgery Initiatives
